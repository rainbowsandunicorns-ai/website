Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers the Google-integrated personal assistant application ("the app") operated by Rainbows & Unicorns AI Ltd ("we", "us"). The app is a private, single-user automation assistant that runs on our own server. It is not a public product and is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

With your explicit Google consent, the app accesses the authorising user's own Google account data via the Google APIs, limited to the scopes you approve, which may include Gmail (read, send and modify your own messages), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Docs and Google Sheets. It only ever accesses the account of the person who authorises it.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to provide personal-assistant features to the authorising user — for example summarising their own email and calendar into a daily briefing, detecting time-sensitive events, and organising their own information. All processing happens on our own private server.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data with any third party.
  • It does not use your data for advertising.
  • It does not transfer your Google data to anyone other than the authorising user.
  • It does not use Google Workspace APIs data to develop, improve or train generalised AI / ML models.

Storage, retention and revocation

Access tokens and any cached data are stored locally on our own server, protected by operating-system file permissions, and retained only as long as needed to provide the assistant features. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party access, which immediately stops all access.

Limited Use (Google API Services User Data Policy)

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For any questions about this policy or the data the app holds, contact hello@rainbowsandunicorns.ai.
